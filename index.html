📋 Before You Go — General Tips

  • Carry originals plus 2 self-attested photocopies of every document
  • Keep 4 recent passport-size photographs (white background)
  • Ensure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar for OTP verification
  • Check office timings before visiting — many offices have specific service hours
  • Keep your phone charged for OTP and digital document verification
