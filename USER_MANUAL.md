# DocReady — User Manual
### Your personal document checklist before every government visit.

---

## 📱 How to Access DocReady

**Website:** Open your browser and go to your Vercel deployment URL  
**Android App:** Install the DocReady APK on your Android phone (coming soon)

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1 — Open the website

Open DocReady in any browser on your phone or computer. No login is required. No personal information is collected.

---

### Step 2 — Select a government service

You will see cards for 6 available services:

| Service | What you can get |
|---|---|
| 🛂 Passport | Fresh application or renewal |
| 🪪 Driving Licence | New licence or renewal |
| 🏠 Ration Card | New card, update, or add a member |
| 🗳️ Voter ID | Fresh registration or correction |
| 📄 Income Certificate | For yourself or your family |
| 📜 Caste Certificate | SC, ST, OBC, or EWS certificate |

You can also type in the search box to find a service quickly.

---

### Step 3 — Select your state (if required)

For **Ration Card**, DocReady will ask you to select your state. This ensures you get the correct list for your state, since ration card requirements vary across states.

---

### Step 4 — Answer 2–3 quick questions

DocReady will ask you a few short questions to personalise your checklist. For example:

- Is this a fresh application or a renewal?
- What is your age group?
- Which category applies to you?

Tap or click the answer that applies to you.

Once all questions are answered, the **Get My Document Checklist** button will become active. Tap it.

---

### Step 5 — Read your personalised checklist

Your personalised document checklist will appear instantly. For each document, you will see:

- ✅ **Document name**
- 📌 **What to carry** — Original, Photocopy, or both
- 💡 **A tip** — where to get it or how to prepare it

Tap or click a document item to mark it as ready. The progress bar at the top will update as you tick off documents.

---

### Step 6 — Read the general tips

Below the checklist, you will see **Before You Go — General Tips**. These are common preparation steps that apply to most government office visits, such as carrying extra photocopies and checking office timings.

---

### Step 7 — Share, copy, or print

Use the buttons at the bottom:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📱 Share on WhatsApp | Opens WhatsApp with the checklist text ready to send |
| 📋 Copy Checklist | Copies the checklist as plain text to your clipboard |
| 🖨️ Print | Opens the browser print dialog with a clean print layout |
| 🔄 Start Over | Goes back to the service selection screen |

---

### Step 8 — Apply on the official portal

After preparing your documents, use the **official portal link** shown at the bottom of the checklist to apply online. DocReady provides direct links to the relevant government portal for each service.

---

## ⚠️ Important

DocReady provides guidance based on standard government requirements. Document requirements may vary by state or local office. **Always verify the final requirements on the official government portal before your visit.**

DocReady is an independent citizen guidance tool. It is not affiliated with any government department.

## 📋 Supported Services

1. Passport (Fresh + Renewal, Adult + Minor, Normal + Tatkal)
2. Driving Licence (Learner, Permanent, Renewal — Two-Wheeler, Four-Wheeler, Both)
3. Ration Card (New, Update, Add Member — APL, BPL, AAY — state-wise)
4. Voter ID (Fresh, Correction, Transfer, Duplicate)
5. Income Certificate (Scholarship, Government Scheme, Legal, Other — Self or Family)
6. Caste Certificate (SC, ST, OBC, EWS — Fresh or Duplicate)
