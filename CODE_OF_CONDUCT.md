# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers of **DocReady**, pledge to make participation in our project and community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

DocReady exists to serve citizens. Everything we build, discuss, and decide should reflect that purpose.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Behaviour we encourage

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and lived experiences
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the citizens who use DocReady
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other contributors
- Crediting others' work and ideas appropriately
- Verifying government document information from official sources before contributing data

### 🚫 Behaviour that is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Offensive comments related to personal identity, background, or experience level
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate misinformation about document requirements (which could harm real citizens)
- Dismissing or belittling contributions without constructive explanation
- Sustained disruptive behaviour in issues, merge requests, or team channels

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying acceptable behaviour and are expected to take fair, consistent corrective action in response to any conduct that violates this Code.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issue tracker, merge requests, team chat channels, and any public spaces where a contributor is representing DocReady (e.g. conference talks, social media posts about the project).

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project team:

📧 **docready-conduct@proton.me** *(replace with your actual contact)*

All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The team will respond with the steps it intends to take.

### Enforcement guidelines

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Minor / first offence | Private written warning with explanation |
| Repeated or moderate | Temporary suspension from project spaces |
| Severe or sustained | Permanent ban from all project spaces |
